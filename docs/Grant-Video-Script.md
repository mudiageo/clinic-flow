# ClinicFlow: 60-Second Grant Pitch Video Script

**Instructions for Recording:**
*   **Setting:** A clean, well-lit background. Have a tablet or laptop open next to you showing the ClinicFlow dashboard.
*   **Tone:** Passionate, confident, and urgent.
*   **Pacing:** Speak clearly but keep the energy high.

---

**(0:00 - 0:15) The Hook**
"70% of Nigerians rely on Primary Health Centres for basic medical needs. But if you visit a rural PHC today, you'll find overwhelming paper records and the 'DHIS2 double-entry trap', where exhausted staff write everything on paper before transcribing it online weeks later. This causes massive delays in detecting disease outbreaks."

**(0:15 - 0:30) The Solution**
"My name is [Your Name], and I built ClinicFlow to solve this. ClinicFlow is an offline-first, AI-powered clinic management system. It captures data instantly at the point of care, working perfectly without the internet by syncing across a local, battery-powered Wi-Fi network."

**(0:30 - 0:45) The AI Magic**
"We also integrated AI to act as a safety net for understaffed clinics. 'Dr. Assist' helps doctors instantly generate structured SOAP notes, while 'RxBrain' automatically analyzes pending prescriptions against patient vitals to prevent fatal drug interactions."

**(0:45 - 1:00) The Ask & Sign-off**
"With a working prototype complete, we are asking for this grant to procure hardware, solar backups, and cover our AI API costs to run our first live pilot in a rural PHC. Together, we can build AI to solve local problems. Thank you."
