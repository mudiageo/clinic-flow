# Using the Permission Editor

The Permission Editor gives the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) and Administrators complete control over what each staff member can see and do in ClinicFlow.

## How it works

When a staff member is invited, they are assigned a **Role** (e.g., Nurse, CHEW). This role comes with a default set of permissions. For example, a Nurse automatically gets access to the General Queue, Vitals, and ANC.

The Permission Editor allows you to **override** these defaults for individual staff members without having to change their main role.

## When to use it

- **Covering Shifts:** If the Pharmacy Tech is absent, you can temporarily grant a Nurse the `Manage Dispensing` and `View Inventory` permissions so they can hand out medications for the day.
- **Specialized Roles:** If you have a CHEW who is exclusively dedicated to field outreach, you can revoke their `View General Queue` permission so their dashboard only shows Field Mode tools.
- **Delegation:** An OIC going on leave can grant a CHO the `Manage Staff` and `Manage PHC` permissions so they can act as OIC temporarily.

## How to use the Editor

1. Go to **Admin > Permission Editor** (or click the Permission Editor tile on your Dashboard).
2. Select a staff member from the list on the left.
3. Review their active permissions on the right. 
   - A standard grey switch means the permission matches their standard role template.
   - A **Blue "Added" badge** means you have granted them an extra permission they normally wouldn't have.
   - A **Red "Revoked" badge** means you have taken away a permission they normally would have.
4. Toggle the switches to add or remove access. The staff member's Dashboard will instantly update the next time they refresh or log in.

## Resetting to Defaults

If a staff member's permissions become too messy, or their temporary shift is over, simply click the **Reset to Defaults** button at the top of their profile. This will instantly wipe all custom additions and revocations, returning them perfectly to their standard role template.
