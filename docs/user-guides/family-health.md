# Family Health

The Family Health workspace groups patient records into PHC-scoped households for coordinated care and outreach.

## Find a household

1. Open **Patient Care → Family Health**.
2. Search by household name, community, or a registered member's name.
3. Select **Open** to view the household dashboard.

The list and summary counts use the device's synchronized offline cache. A record marked **pending** is waiting to sync; **conflict** requires review in the Sync Engine.

## Household dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- all linked patient records;
- active reminders for household members;
- upcoming scheduled visits;
- the household's recorded community.

Select a member to open their patient profile.

## Create or link a family

From a patient's profile, use the family-linking control to find an existing relative or create a new household. New households inherit the patient's PHC and community and are queued for durable synchronization when offline.

## Privacy and access

Only staff with the **Manage Families** permission can access family-management data. Household records are restricted to the staff member's assigned PHC. Do not use family grouping to infer relationships that the patient or guardian has not confirmed.

This feature does not use AI and does not provide clinical recommendations.
